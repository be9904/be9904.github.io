# Adventure in Dream Land

A short, scroll-controlled side-scrolling story about my childhood, told
through Kirby. Kirby walks, runs and jumps through the countries I grew up
in, and a caption under the screen narrates each stage of my life.

Live page: <https://be9904.github.io/kirby/>

## How to play

- Press **Start** (or Enter) to begin. The music starts with it.
- **Scroll down** to walk forward. **Scroll faster** to run.
- **Scroll up** to walk back the way you came.
- Kirby keeps a little momentum, so it coasts to a stop when you stop scrolling.
- Jumps and the moves between scenes play on their own. Just keep scrolling.
- Use the speaker icon in the top-right corner to mute or unmute the music.
- At the end, press **Play again** to start over.

## Scenes

## What was made with generative AI

- **Scene images.** All six backgrounds (the intro landscape, China, Taiwan,
  Korea, Cambodia and Back to Korea) were generated with an AI image model.
  The intro image is reused for the last scene.
- **Foreground layers.** The grass in the intro and the poles, wires, railing
  and scooters in Back to Korea are cut out of those same images so Kirby can
  walk behind them. The cut-outs were made with AI-written code.
- **The website.** All of the code (HTML, CSS and JavaScript) was written with
  an AI coding assistant. This includes the scroll controls, Kirby's movement
  and jumps, the scene transitions, the music toggle and the image loading.
  The muted-speaker icon was also made this way.

## What was not made with AI

- **The captions.** Every line of narration was written by me.
- **The story.** The places, the order and what each one meant to me are my
  own experiences.
- **Kirby's sprites** come from a sprite sheet ripped by A.J. Nitro. Kirby is
  a character owned by Nintendo and HAL Laboratory.
- **The music** is "Vegetable Valley" from *Kirby: Squeak Squad*.

## A personal note

The images and the code were generated with AI, but I tried my best to express
my own experiences through them. Each scene comes from a place I actually lived.
I chose how each place should look and how Kirby should move through it, and I
wrote every caption myself. AI was the tool I used to build it, but the story
is mine.
